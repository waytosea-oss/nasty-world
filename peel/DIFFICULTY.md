# Difficulty curve (measured)

Measured with `measure.html`: a simulated player peels every scene with no tools unlocked, a 1.3 s pause between strips, ±20% speed jitter and a 0.12–0.32 s reaction time at the SNIP prompt.
Player types: **careful** = 250 u/s, slows near the end and stops at SNIP; **normal** = 400 u/s, eases off a little; **greedy** = 600 u/s and keeps dragging.

| Scene | careful | normal | greedy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sunday Novel | WIN 100% · 30.7s · 0 ouch | WIN 100% · 25.7s · 0 ouch | lose 56% · 17.8s · 3 ouch |
| 2. Deadline Laptop | WIN 100% · 37.9s · 0 ouch | WIN 100% · 37.2s · 0 ouch | lose 37% · 22s · 3 ouch |
| 3. Beach Aftermath | WIN 100% · 19.2s · 0 ouch | WIN 100% · 23.2s · 2 ouch | lose 71% · 39.2s · 3 ouch |
| 4. The Throne Scroll | WIN 100% · 45s · 0 ouch | lose 60% · 42.7s · 3 ouch | lose 19% · 14s · 3 ouch |
| 5. Nature Doc Dream | WIN 100% · 49.4s · 0 ouch | lose 36% · 23.7s · 3 ouch | lose 21% · 17.9s · 3 ouch |
| 6. Midnight Monster (boss) | WIN 100% · 91.8s · 0 ouch | lose 36% · 49.5s · 3 ouch | lose 19% · 49.8s · 3 ouch |

Takeaways: careful play clears every scene (the boss only just makes it, 92 s of 90+). Normal play clears scenes 1–3 and fails from scene 4 on (sticky, crumbly skin, calluses, interruptions). Greedy play always ends in OUCHes. Real players are slower than the bot, which puts a typical scene at about 60–90 s. Unlockable tools (clippers, soak, pumice) give later scenes extra slack.
