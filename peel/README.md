# Heelarious: The Great Flake-Off

A satisfying, crude-humor (18+) foot-peeling mini-game for Nasty World. A guy is absorbed in a book, laptop, TV or phone and idly picks his own dry foot skin. You do the picking.

- Grab a glowing flake edge and drag along the strip. The skin is a verlet soft-body strip that folds back, crunches and tears if you pull faster than the skin allows. Sticky green spots are weaker.
- At the end of a strip, let go during the SNIP window for a PERFECT SNIP. Keep dragging and you go "too deep": OUCH plus a comic red dot and band-aid. Three OUCHes and the scene ends.
- His face reacts live: hums when you peel well, winces near the edge, giggles and jerks his foot when you linger on ticklish spots.
- Surprises: beach sand, sock lint and lost coins under the skin, and a rare golden MEGA SHEET (×3). Finishing a strip reveals the next tip so peels chain.
- Per-scene mechanics: sunburn sheets, calluses to scrub (pumice), crumbly athlete's foot, elephant plates, and a boss foot whose skin regrows.
- Interruptions (phone buzz, the cat, "Are you picking your feet AGAIN?!"): FREEZE or lose your combo.
- Stars unlock the next scene and tools: clippers, soak, magnifier, pumice.
- Long strips (≥20 cm) trigger slow-mo and a cheer. A PNG share card is generated automatically.
- All sounds are synthesised with WebAudio. Vibration is used where available, with screen-shake as the fallback. No network calls, no analytics, no popups. Progress is saved in localStorage.

Fonts are OFL (see fonts/OFL.txt). Logo options are in shots/ (A = chosen).
